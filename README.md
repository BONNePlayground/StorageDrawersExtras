## Storage Drawers Extra: Reloaded

This is an updated [Storage Drawers Extra](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/storage-drawers-extras) addon pack for the Storage Drawers mod, updated and expanded for Minecraft 1.21.1.
It adds new wooden drawer styles and crafting recipes using plank blocks from other mods - letting your storage match the unique woods from your favorite modpacks.

#### 🧱 What’s New in the Reloaded Edition
- Updated for Minecraft 1.21.1
- Now supports Forge, NeoForge, and Fabric

Maintains the original aesthetic and compatibility goals

#### 🌲 Currently Supported Mods
- Biomes O’ Plenty [fabric, forge and neoforge]
- Immersive Engineering [neoforge]
- Oh The Biomes We’ve Been [fabric, forge and neoforge]

#### 📦 About Storage Drawers

Storage Drawers puts your most frequently used items within convenient reach. It borrows ideas from Jabba and Bibliocraft, resulting in a highly functional, compact, and aesthetic storage solution designed to complement - not replace - those mods.
